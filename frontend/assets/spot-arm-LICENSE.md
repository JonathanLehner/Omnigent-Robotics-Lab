# Spot arm landing-page render

spot-arm.webp is an offline MuJoCo render of the arm geometry from Google DeepMind's MuJoCo Menagerie boston_dynamics_spot/spot_arm.xml, also used by this lab through robot_descriptions. The torso and legs are omitted for the landing composition. The image is illustrative; it is not a live simulation.

Model source: https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/main/boston_dynamics_spot
